# Supplementary Fig. R1.7: six-tumor models on HPRC v2.1, training curves

## Caption (draft)

**Supplementary Fig. R1.7 | Training curves of the six-tumor models on the HPRC v2.1 graph.** One run per platform, trained on chr2–22 of HCC1395T, HCC1954T, H1437T, H2009T, HG008T and COLO829T (chr1 of every tumor and HCC1395T chr20 held out). **a**, SNV models: 50.4 M parameters, trained for 20 epochs; 278,615–288,202 somatic, 1.94–2.25 M germline and 0.59–24.8 M other training examples. **b**, INDEL models: 8.7 M parameters, trained for 12 epochs; 14,132–14,819 somatic, 0.44–1.22 M germline and 0.47–15.6 M other. Top rows: class-weighted cross-entropy on the training set (mean over each epoch) and on the validation set (5% of the 20,000-node blocks of the training chromosomes, disjoint from training). Bottom rows: validation somatic F1 after our population filter, the metric that selects the checkpoint (ONT SNVs: before the filter). Dotted line: selected epoch; curves are shown up to 2 epochs after it. At the selected epoch the validation loss is 1.01–1.10 times the lowest of the run. Later epochs may overfit the training data, so their checkpoints are not used.

Generated by `make_figure.py` from each run's `train.log`, `args.json`, `metrics.jsonl` (paths below). Losses are class-weighted cross-entropy (training: mean over the epoch's batches; validation: after the epoch). Selection metric: `postpon` = validation somatic F1 after our population filter; `f1` = before it. Examples = candidate sites (tensors) on chr2-22 (HCC1395T without chr20); validation = 5% of the 20,000-node blocks, disjoint from training; chr1 is in neither.
