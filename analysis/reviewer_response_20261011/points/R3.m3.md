**Response.**

We thank the reviewer. The original Methods named the reference only as an "allele-frequency-filtered HPRC pangenome graph"; the revised Methods specify each graph:

- **HPRC v1.1 graph (d9 filter):** HPRC release 1, built with Minigraph-Cactus on GRCh38 (Liao et al., 2023); 44 individuals (African 23, Admixed American 16, East Asian 4, South Asian 1, no European) plus GRCh38 and CHM13, 90 haplotypes in all.
- **HPRC v2.1 graph (d46 filter):** HPRC release 2, built the same way (Lucas et al., 2026); 231 individuals (African 70, East Asian 50, Admixed American 44, South Asian 36, European 29, plus HG002 and HG005) plus GRCh38 and CHM13, 464 haplotypes in all.
- **Frequency filter:** both graphs are used as the HPRC distributes them. The filter removes nodes and edges supported by fewer than 9 of 90 (d9) or 46 of 464 (d46) haplotypes, about 10%; the GRCh38 and CHM13 reference paths are never filtered.
- **GRCh38-only graph** (R1.2, R3.1): built with vg autoindex from the GRCh38 no-alt analysis set.

**Structural variants** pass the same filter, and those kept are graph paths that reads can follow. Pansoma calls only SNVs and INDELs of 1–50 bp; longer events are not called.

**Benchmark individuals.** None of the six cancer lines (HG008, COLO829, HCC1395, HCC1954, H1437, H2009) is in either graph's sample list. Because a sample list cannot exclude relatives, we also tested the HG008, COLO829 and HCC1395 normals genetically: of their germline SNVs carried by only one or two haplotypes of a release, no HPRC individual carries more than 8.2% (v1.1) or 2.9% (v2.1), whereas a first-degree relative would carry about half. The other three lines were checked by sample list only. The HapMap mixture donors, in contrast, are HPRC individuals; that experiment uses a graph without them (R1.5).

Although the HG008 donor is of European ancestry, which v1.1 lacks, the two graphs give close results on HG008T after population filtering (R1.2, R3.1). "AF-HPRC" and "AF-Graph" (original Fig. 2e and Fig. 5) are now "HPRC v1.1 graph (d9 filter)" throughout.
