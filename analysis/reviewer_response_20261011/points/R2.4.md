**Response.**

*Thresholds.* A candidate now needs an allele fraction of at least 0.08 and at least three ALT-supporting reads (base quality ≥ 10), for SNVs and INDELs alike. Below this floor a variant never reaches the classifier; on chr1 of undiluted COLO829T this concerns 15.1–16.1% of the truth SNVs.

*Sensitivity by VAF.* COLO829T, chr1, benchmark regions (for COLO829T the whole genome), HPRC v2.1 graph (d46 filter), three platforms, PASS calls after population filtering. A truth SNV's VAF is its allele fraction in the tumor reads, or the SMaHT truth VAF when it has no candidate:

| truth VAF | truth SNVs | recall | recall of those with a candidate |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 0.05 | 284–301 | 0 | – |
| 0.05–0.10 | 139–176 | 0.230–0.348 | 0.853–0.941 |
| 0.10–0.20 | 149–177 | 0.672–0.897 | 0.850–0.966 |
| 0.20–0.30 | 59–88 | 0.763–0.853 | 0.804–0.889 |
| 0.30–0.50 | 266–391 | 0.808–0.857 | 0.840–0.861 |
| ≥ 0.50 | 1,356–1,507 | 0.836–0.857 | 0.841–0.867 |

Recall falls below 50% in the 0.05–0.10 bin, where only 34–68 truth SNVs have a candidate; once a candidate exists, recall is 0.804–0.966 in every bin. HG008T has too few low-VAF truth SNVs on chr1 for this analysis.

*Decreasing purity.* For each platform, one model was trained on chr2–22 of COLO829T and its in-silico dilutions with COLO829BL (nominal purity 75, 50, 25%) and tested on chr1 of each purity at one recall-0.9 validation threshold shared by all purities; training and test share the tumor (one seed). On Fiber-seq, after population filtering and in the benchmark regions (graph-coordinate calls not scored; R3.4), SNV recall is 0.698 / 0.639 / 0.621 / 0.663 from 100% to 25% purity, at precision 0.881 / 0.741 / 0.715 / 0.786, while the share of truth SNVs whose expected VAF is below the 0.08 floor rises from 16.1% to 26.9%. At 25% purity, 29.3% of the truth SNVs have no candidate (18.4% undiluted), while recall is close to the highest the curve reaches after filtering (0.663 against 0.688), so most of the loss occurs at candidate generation; by VAF it is 0.899 at 0.10–0.20 (829 of 922 truth SNVs) and 0.420 at 0.05–0.10. At 75% and 50% the classifier loses more (curve maxima 0.767 / 0.740) and precision falls: 86.1% and 86.5% of the false calls carry a germline allele of the normal (68.4% undiluted), as expected when germline SNVs in the tumor's regions of loss of heterozygosity enter the somatic allele-fraction range (R1.5). Illumina and ONT dilutions carry a read-source base-quality cue that can make their scores optimistic; R1.5 reports both without it. For ONT the effect is small: without the cue, SNV F1 is 0.724 / 0.693 / 0.666 / 0.700 from 100% to 25% purity, against 0.733 / 0.721 / 0.691 / 0.714 with it. These low-purity results come from one tumor.

*Below the candidate floor.* The dilutions reach truth VAFs of about 0.05. To measure sensitivity below the 0.08 floor, we reran the SMaHT HAPMAP6 mixture (about 500x; we evaluate minor-donor alleles at VAF 0.01–0.165) with the current pipeline on a graph built without its six donors (R1.5), with a candidate floor of 0.015 (about eight ALT reads at this depth), and trained one model per variant type on chr2–22. Its positives are germline alleles of other individuals, so it gives the empirical sensitivity by VAF in this mixture, not somatic accuracy. We score chr1 without population filtering, which would remove many of these germline positives (R2.1), and leave out positives carried by a path of the graph (mostly alleles shared by several donors), from training as well as evaluation: their reads align without an edit, which concerns at most 2% of the HG008T and COLO829T somatic truth SNVs on the HPRC v2.1 graph (R1.2). [待更新: E23 retrained and re-evaluated without graph-carried positives (jobs 460499–460503)] At the recall-0.9 validation threshold, SNV recall by truth VAF is 0.30 / 0.81 / 0.91 at VAF 0.01 / 0.02–0.025 / 0.05–0.075, and precision by the calls' own allele fraction is 0.98–0.99 in every bin; in these bins, 85–95% of the truth SNVs that reach the classifier are called. INDEL recall is 0.12 / 0.19 / 0.37 at the same VAFs (precision 0.62–0.88 by call allele fraction). These precisions are likely optimistic: 35% of the chr1 positives lie within 50 bp of another positive, against 4% of COLO829T somatic variants, so reads often carry several donor alleles at once. These results replace the HapMap results of the submitted Supplementary Table 3 (SNV recall 0.94 at VAF 0.01–0.165), which are not comparable: that recall was computed against a truth set of 96,393 SNVs in this VAF range, whereas the truth set used here has 5.17 million genome-wide (392,566 on chr1).

*The low-VAF model.* The original low-VAF model was the same kind of network trained on the HapMap mixture, with SNV candidates built at an allele fraction of 0.015 instead of 0.10 and tensors of up to 500 reads instead of 200; the submitted Methods described an allele-fraction window of 1–10%, and the revised Methods give the settings used. Its positives were germline alleles, so it is withdrawn as a somatic model (R1.5). The HapMap models above use the same floor with the current pipeline (200 reads per tensor) and serve only to measure this sensitivity. To see how this configuration behaves on real somatic variants, we applied the HapMap models without retraining to chr1 of the COLO829T Illumina dilutions, built with the same 0.015 floor [待补: E15b recall by truth VAF and precision, before and after population filtering, against the default model].

*Usage.* We recommend the default model for expected somatic VAFs of about 0.10 and above, where call-level recall in the 0.10–0.20 bin is 0.672–0.897 in undiluted COLO829T and 0.899 at 25% purity. No truth SNV below VAF 0.05 was called by the default model. Lower VAFs need both a lower candidate floor and depth: three ALT reads correspond to VAF 0.03 at 100x, and below about 0.005 a true allele (one to three reads even at 500x) is hard to separate from sequencing errors in a single sample.
