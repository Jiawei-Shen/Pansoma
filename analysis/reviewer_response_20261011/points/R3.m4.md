**Response.**

We thank the reviewer and have checked terminology and language throughout the manuscript and Supplement.

- **Population filtering.** We agree. The step is now called "population filtering", and gnomAD, dbSNP, CoLoRSdb and the GATK 1000 Genomes panel of normals are the "population filtering resources". "Panel of normals" now refers only to the GATK file, the one panel of normals in the usual sense among the four, and "PoN" no longer names all four. Methods list each resource and its matching rule (R2.6, R3.m1).
- **NPU.** The original expanded NPU in three different ways. The revised pipeline no longer writes NPU files: Giraffe alignments are sorted by node ID and indexed with vg gamsort, and the reads at each candidate node are fetched from this indexed GAM. The term NPU and its format table (Supplementary Table 1) are removed.
- **Names.** The method is "Pansoma" throughout (Supplementary Tables 4 and 5 used "Pansoma-TO"), and supplementary items are cited as "Supplementary Fig." and "Supplementary Table". Recurring terms are defined once in Methods: "GRCh38-only graph", "HPRC v1.1 graph (d9 filter)" and "HPRC v2.1 graph (d46 filter)" (replacing "AF-HPRC" and "AF-Graph"); "Illumina" rather than "WGS" for short reads; "graph-coordinate calls"; "candidate" and "candidate site" (R2.m8); and "the recall-0.9 validation threshold" and "the validation best-F1 threshold".
- **Quoted passages.** These now read "One key challenge in calling variants from graph alignments is that these alignments differ fundamentally from linear-reference alignments", "Pansoma converts the variants …", "with the technical details described in Methods" and "read pileup" (Fig. 5).
- **Language.** The revised manuscript was edited for language by [待补: who did the language edit].
