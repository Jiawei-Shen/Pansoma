**Response.**

We added four of these lines: HCC1395 with the SEQC2 v1.2.1 truth set, and HCC1954, H1437 and H2009 from the CASTLE panel of the DeepSomatic study, each sequenced with Illumina, PacBio HiFi and ONT (R2.2). All twelve datasets were processed with the same pipeline on the HPRC v2.1 graph and used for multi-tumor training and cross-tumor tests (R1.7).

**Comparison with DeepSomatic-TO and ClairS-TO.** The SEQC2 truth is independent of all three callers. DeepSomatic-TO, like Pansoma's multi-tumor models, was trained on HCC1395 with chr1 held out; ClairS-TO was never trained on HCC1395. With the same truth, benchmark regions and population filter, chr1 SNV F1 of PASS calls (Illumina / PacBio / ONT) is 0.845 / 0.820 / 0.658 for the multi-tumor model trained on the four lines and 0.850 / 0.825 / 0.730 with HG008T and COLO829T chr2–22 added, against 0.848 / 0.839 / 0.760 for DeepSomatic-TO and 0.832 / 0.814 / 0.760 for ClairS-TO (Supplementary Table [X]). The four-line model, which never saw HG008T, reaches 0.605 / 0.614 / 0.587 on it, above DeepSomatic-TO (0.529 / 0.557 / 0.457) and ClairS-TO (0.552 / 0.591 / 0.552); on the unseen, UV-dominated COLO829T it falls below both on PacBio and ONT (R1.7). For HCC1395 INDELs, DeepSomatic-TO and ClairS-TO reach 0.605 / 0.612 / 0.471 and 0.641 / 0.566 / 0.569; [待补: Pansoma multi-tumor INDEL F1 on HCC1395 chr1 with the final INDEL model].

**No tool comparison on the CASTLE lines.** Their truth sets were derived from DeepSomatic calls, which its authors note biases them toward DeepSomatic, and these lines are in the training data of DeepSomatic's multi-cancer models and of ClairS-TO's real-tumor (SSRS) models. We therefore use them only to train and test Pansoma.

**HCC1937 and Hs578T** were not added: both are breast cancer lines, a type already represented by HCC1395 and HCC1954, and each additional line adds three whole-genome datasets to process and train on.
