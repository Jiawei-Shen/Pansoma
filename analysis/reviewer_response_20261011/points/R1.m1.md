**Response.**

We added four of these lines: HCC1395 (SEQC2 v1.2.1 truth) and three CASTLE lines of the DeepSomatic study, HCC1954 (breast) and H1437 and H2009 (lung adenocarcinoma), each with Illumina, PacBio HiFi and ONT data processed on the HPRC v2.1 graph (R2.2). Because every line adds three whole-genome datasets, we took three of the five CASTLE lines, covering both cancer types of the panel.

**The CASTLE lines serve as training data, not as benchmarks.** With one method for all tumors, we measured how many somatic truth alleles are already alleles of the pangenome, that is, carried by at least 46 of the 463 HPRC v2.1 haplotypes and therefore kept in the graph we use. For truth SNVs, this share is 0.15–1.9% for COLO829T, HCC1395, HG008T and HCC1954, but 4.6% for H2009 and 24.5% for H1437 (truth INDELs: 39.5% for H1437, against 3.0–19.1% for the other five). In H1437 and H2009, 99% and 91% of these alleles lie in segments where the B-lymphoblastoid normal has lost heterozygosity. At 21 such sites (Supplementary Table [X]), the tumor carries a common population allele (carried by 47–386 HPRC haplotypes) on 11–71 PacBio reads, whereas the normal shows 0–5 ALT reads among 22–95. A new mutation rarely recreates a common population allele, so these are most likely inherited alleles lost from the normal cell line, which the tumor–normal comparison labeled somatic. In training on the graph, these alleles do little harm: reads carrying them follow the graph path, so 99.1–99.7% of H1437's 18,028 such SNVs produce no candidate, and they make up at most 0.11% of the somatic SNV training examples of the three CASTLE lines (at most 2.3% for INDELs). In an evaluation, however, they would count as missed somatic variants: population filtering tags all but two of them, which alone would cap the recall of every caller on H1437 at about 75%. In addition, the CASTLE truth sets were derived from DeepSomatic calls, and these lines are in both comparators' training data. We therefore evaluate on HCC1395, whose independent SEQC2 truth shows no such excess, and on HG008T and COLO829T.

**Results** (chr1, SNV F1 after population filtering, Illumina / PacBio / ONT; R1.7). A model trained on HCC1395 (without chr1 and chr20) and the three CASTLE lines reaches 0.845 / 0.820 / 0.658 on HCC1395, against 0.848 / 0.839 / 0.760 for DeepSomatic-TO, which was also trained on HCC1395 with chr1 held out, and 0.832 / 0.814 / 0.760 for ClairS-TO. On HG008T, which this model never saw, it reaches 0.605 / 0.614 / 0.587, above DeepSomatic-TO (0.529 / 0.557 / 0.457) and ClairS-TO (0.552 / 0.591 / 0.552); on the UV-dominated COLO829T, also unseen, it reaches 0.671 / 0.595 / 0.380. Adding chr2–22 of HG008T and COLO829T (six tumors) raises HCC1395 to 0.850 / 0.825 / 0.730 and COLO829T to 0.755 / 0.774 / 0.737, close to the COLO829T-specific models (0.783 / 0.779 / 0.736).
