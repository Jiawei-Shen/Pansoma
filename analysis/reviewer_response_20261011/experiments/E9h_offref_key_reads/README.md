# E9h: read-level check of the assembly off-reference somatic SNV keys

Reviewer points: **R1.1** (does the pangenome / channel 6 matter) and **R3.3** (validation of off-reference somatic
variants). E1.1 (`../E1.1_ch6_counterfactual/README.md`, section 5) showed that the trained SNV models call almost
none of the assembly off-reference somatic SNV keys, and that the off-reference rescue (`site10_high`, argmax) and
`all_H` call many of them. Those keys come from the HG008-N v6.2 assembly and the HG008-T truth, not from reads. This
experiment asks: **do the reads that carry each key's allele support a somatic allele, and how many of the truly
somatic keys does the rescue recover?**

Status: **DONE** (2026-10-15). Every number below is in `tables.md` (generated by `classify.py`; section numbers
refer to it) or `results/summary.json`.

## Answer

Read-supported somatic = `tumor_only`: the consensus of the key's alt-supporting (A1) reads occurs in the HG008-T v3.2
assembly and not in HG008-N v6.2. Two versions of the test (section 0):

| set | keys (chr1) | tumor-only, h30 earlier (chr1) | base / rescue / all_H on them | tumor-only, h50 strict (chr1) | base / rescue / all_H on them | rescue calls on all keys: tumor-only (h50 strict) / all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `HG008T_PacBio` (d9) | 112 (8) | 21 (4) | 0 / 7 / 14 | 72 (7) | 0 / 22 / 43 | 22 / 32 |
| `HG008T_ONT` (d9) | 89 (8) | 24 (4) | 0 / 13 / 14 | 51 (6) | 0 / 29 / 30 | 29 / 46 |
| `HG008T_Illumina_d46` | 135 (12) | 28 (5) | 2 / 21 / 23 | 93 (10) | 3 / 66 / 76 | 66 / 80 |
| `HG008T_PacBio_d46` | 82 (11) | 23 (4) | 1 / 13 / 18 | 65 (9) | 1 / 33 / 50 | 33 / 38 |
| `HG008T_ONT_d46` | 67 (9) | 21 (3) | 4 / 14 / 19 | 50 (7) | 4 / 32 / 40 | 32 / 40 |
| `HG008T_Illumina` (d9; no E1.1 scores) | 184 (10) | 32 (4) | - | 115 (9) | - | - |

base = trained model at the PASS threshold t (validation recall 0.9); rescue = `predict`'s off-reference rescue
(`site10_high`, called by argmax); all_H = channel 6 set to H, at t. All autosomes; chr1 in brackets.

- **`h30 earlier`** is the requested method of `offref_negatives_20260929` (61-bp query, a hit may contain a 1-bp
  indel). It reproduces the earlier HG008T Illumina d9 result (32 tumor-only of 184 here; 30 there, section 5).
- **`h50 strict`** uses the whole 101-column tensor and does not let a repeat-length difference count as a hit (the
  E9d rule). **This is the version to cite.** The earlier test cannot see these events: they are mostly repeat-length
  changes (INDEL in the assembly frame for 181 of the 256 events; 44-128 keys per set, 64-73 %, in a homopolymer of
  at least 7 bp, section 1). On the events' own somatic allele contexts, which are somatic by construction,
  `h30 earlier` returns tumor-only for only 49 of 256 events (11 of 181 INDEL events) and normal for 187. `h50 strict` returns tumor-only
  for 218 of 256 (151 of 181 INDEL, 67 of 75 SNV events). On the germline (N) contexts it returns normal for 253 of
  256 and tumor-only for 1 (section 5, controls).
- **For R1.1 / R3.3:** with `h50 strict`, 57-79 % of the keys are read-supported somatic (72 / 112 PacBio d9,
  51 / 89 ONT d9, 93 / 135 Illumina d46, 65 / 82 PacBio d46, 50 / 67 ONT d46). The trained models call at most 4 of
  them at the PASS threshold: 0, 0, 3, 1, 4. The rescue (`site10_high` argmax) recovers 22, 29, 66, 33 and 32, and
  `all_H` 43, 30, 76, 50 and 40 at t. Of the rescue's calls on these keys, 22 / 32, 29 / 46, 66 / 80, 33 / 38 and
  32 / 40 are read-supported somatic. On chr1 (8-12 keys per set) the rescue recovers 3, 4, 8, 2 and 4 tumor-only keys
  (section 3, `h50_strict`).
- The earlier conclusion that most of these keys are "germline haplotypes in repeats" (only 30 tumor-only for HG008T
  Illumina d9) is mostly an effect of the hit rule and the 61-bp window, not of the reads. With `h50 strict` the same HG008T
  Illumina d9 keys are 115 / 184 tumor-only.

## Method

1. **Query** (`build_queries.py`; the consensus of `offref_negatives_20260929/v1_queries.py`, adapted here, the
   original unchanged). Take the key's tensor and its A1 rows (`row_groups`). For each column, take the most frequent
   read base (channel 0) over the A1 rows that cover it (channel 4 > 0), when at least 2 rows cover it. The query is
   the contiguous run of such columns around the site column (50), up to HALF columns on each side; gap columns add
   no base. HALF = 30 (the earlier method, <= 61 bp) and HALF = 50 (the whole 101-column tensor). Every key got a
   query at both lengths (section 1). At the site, every covering A1 row carries the key's ALT (`site_alt_frac` 1.0
   everywhere). For HG008T Illumina d9 all 184 h30 queries are identical to the earlier run's queries (section 5).
2. **Mapping** (`jobs/map.sh`): `minimap2 2.28 -ax sr --secondary=yes -N 20 --cs` (the earlier settings) of the key
   queries and the events' N / T windows to each of the four assembly haplotypes separately.
3. **Hit rules** (`classify.py`): a primary or secondary alignment covering >= 90 % of the query with NM <= 1, and
   - `earlier` (`offref_negatives_20260929/v2_classify.py`, adapted): the site base lies in a match block. A 1-bp
     insertion or deletion elsewhere in the query is allowed.
   - `strict` (`E9c_offref_filter_validation/e9d_asm.py`, adapted): no insertion or deletion anywhere, and no
     mismatch within one base of the site.
4. **Event locus**: the alignments of the event's N and T windows (>= 90 % aligned, NM <= 5) +- 100 bp in each
   haplotype, plus the event's own HG008-N position +- 200 bp.
5. **Classes** (per query length and rule):
   - `tumor_only`: a hit in HG008-T and none in HG008-N. The key is read-supported somatic.
   - `normal`: a hit in HG008-N. The A1 haplotype is the patient's germline sequence.
   - `neither`: no hit in either assembly.
   - `ambiguous`: no query, or a query that hits HG008-N only away from the event's locus while it hits HG008-T at
     the locus. In the second case the 61 / 101 bases also occur elsewhere in the normal genome, so the tensor window
     cannot tell a somatic allele here from a germline copy elsewhere (`paralog`).
6. **Controls** (`build_controls.py`, `jobs/map_controls.sh`): the same rules on the events' own allele contexts,
   taken from the 201-bp windows as the allele +- 30 / +- 50 bases. The T context is somatic by construction and the N
   context is germline by construction.
7. **Calls**: E1.1's per-key probabilities with each run's threshold t. The recount equals E1.1's own assembly summary
   for all five runs (section 5).

## Results in more detail (`tables.md`)

- **Classes (section 2).** `h30 earlier`: tumor-only 21 / 112 (PacBio d9), 24 / 89 (ONT d9), 28 / 135, 23 / 82,
  21 / 67 (d46 Illumina / PacBio / ONT); `normal` 84, 57, 92, 53, 42. `h50 strict`: tumor-only 72, 51, 93, 65, 50;
  `normal` 28, 21, 22, 8, 6; `neither` 11, 17, 17, 8, 11; `ambiguous` 1, 0, 3, 1, 0. Changing only one of the two
  settings gives counts in between: `h30 strict` tumor-only 45, 41, 62, 48, 40; `h50 earlier` 40, 34, 50, 33, 29.
- **Why (section 4).** Under `h30 earlier`, every HG008-N hit of 19-37 `normal` keys per set contains an indel.
  These are repeat-length differences between the A1 reads and the normal genome, which the earlier rule counts as a
  match. Going from `h30 earlier` to `h30 strict` moves 20-38 keys per set from normal to tumor-only. Among INDEL
  events, `h30 earlier` gives tumor-only for 4-9 keys per set and `h30 strict` for 21-43 (section 4, strata). The
  61-bp window can also miss the change entirely (examples from `results/key_classes.tsv` and `queries.fa`, PacBio d9).
  In e02003 (TA>T in a 13-bp homopolymer), key 12663481:0:SNP:C>G lies more than 30 bases from the homopolymer: the
  h30 query does not contain it, the h50 query does. In e02438 (a 12-bp deletion in a CA repeat), the h30 query ends
  inside the repeat.
- **The remaining `normal` keys (h50 strict).** Most `normal:at_locus` keys hit the event's own HG008-N haplotype or
  both haplotypes (section 4). For 22 of 28 (PacBio d9) and 15 of 21 (ONT d9) of them, the event's own somatic context
  is tumor-only while the key's reads are not. These reads carry germline sequence, not the event's allele. The other
  keys (6 / 6) belong to events whose somatic context also occurs in HG008-N (section 5, key class against event T
  context). Those cannot be separated from germline at the sequence level.
- **`neither` (h50 strict)** 8-17 keys per set: the read consensus is in neither assembly. For all of them except
  at most one per set, the event's own T context is tumor-only (section 5). This fits consensus errors such as
  long-read homopolymer lengths, or an A1 block that mixes alleles. These keys are not counted as supported.
- **Earlier run reproduced (section 5).** HG008T Illumina d9, `h30 earlier` against `offref_negatives_20260929`
  (old | here): normal | normal 117, normal | ambiguous 4, normal | neither 1, tumor | tumor_only 26, tumor | normal 4,
  neither | normal 14, neither | neither 9, neither | tumor_only 6, neither | ambiguous 3. The queries are identical. The differences come from mapping each haplotype separately, which finds hits that the
  earlier both-haplotype index missed (mostly multi-copy homopolymer queries).
- **E9d agreement (section 5).** E9d's independent assembly class of the same keys uses a 101-bp `alt_read`, the strict
  rule, minimap2 -N 50 and both-haplotype indexes. It agrees with `h50 strict`: e.g. PacBio d9 E9d tumor_only 70 ->
  here tumor_only 69 / neither 1, E9d germline 26 -> normal 25 / ambiguous 1; PacBio d46 tumor_only 66 -> 65 / 1,
  germline 11 -> normal 8 / neither 2 / ambiguous 1.
- **The events themselves (section 5).** The T window is exact in HG008-T for 253 of the 256 events (NM <= 1: 256)
  and exact in HG008-N for 1. The N window is exact in HG008-N for all 256.

## Caveats

- `tumor_only` means "in the tumor assembly, not in the normal assembly", for one consensus haplotype per key. It
  does not prove that every A1 read is somatic: when somatic and germline reads mix in the A1 block, the consensus
  follows the majority.
- The strict rule never counts a repeat-length difference as normal. A germline read consensus with a homopolymer
  length error becomes `neither`, unless HG008-T has exactly that sequence and HG008-N does not. On the germline
  controls this produced 1 tumor-only of 256 (h50 strict; 3 of 256 at h30).
- The A1 consensus is projected onto graph columns: inserted read bases outside the site are not in the query.
- In repeats, minimap2 reports at most 21 alignments per haplotype (`-N 20`). The at-locus copy can be missing,
  which affects `normal:elsewhere` against `ambiguous:paralog`. Some 61-bp germline controls were missed altogether
  (N h30 contexts: 243 of 256 normal).
- Illumina d9 has no E1.1 scores (E1.1 did not score the assembly keys for that model).
